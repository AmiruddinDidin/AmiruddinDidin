### 🚀 Projects

**Spacecraft**

- **Propellant-Free Collision Avoidance for Small Satellites**: a bi-directional electrodynamic tether governed by nonlinear model predictive control (NMPC), verified against analytical orbit-attitude dynamics and real space-debris catalogue data (SGP4). *1st Winner, AIC 2026.*

**UAVs** (with Aksantara ITB)

- **AKSA VIKARA**: hybrid VTOL mothership deploying a swarm of 5-inch child drones for search and rescue. Sensor integration, PID tuning, telemetry in a Python ground station. *1st Place, KRTI 2025.*
- **AMBALABU**: vision-based precision target striking, with a YOLOv11 dataset I labelled. *Best Potential Place, KIDN 2025.*
- **Man Machine Drone**: a glove controller (IMU, copper-fabric finger contacts, Arduino Pro Micro, ELRS) for piloting pick-up-and-deliver missions. *SAFMC 2026.*
- **High Speed Drone Flock**: three autonomous indoor drones; avionics and safety pilot. *SAFMC 2026.*
- **Fixed Wing Drone**: vision-based precision payload dropping on a Raspberry Pi 5. *Teknofest 2026.*

**SPH simulations** (personal)

- **Big Bang Cosmology**: N-body + SPH from the electroweak epoch to the dark-energy era, 66,923 particles on an RTX 3050.
- **Milky Way and Andromeda Merger**: N-body + SPH galaxy collision with live separation, star-formation and energy graphs.
- **Rinjani Landslide**: 3D SPH (CUDA, 111,843 particles) of a hypothetical rockslide-triggered outburst flood from Segara Anak.
- **Moon Impact on Earth**: SPH (CUDA, 29,186 particles) of a 10.1 km/s impact at 45°.
